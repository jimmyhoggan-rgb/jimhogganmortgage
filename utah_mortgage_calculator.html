Buydown cost assumptions:
Temporary buydown costs (2-1 and 1-0) are estimated as the total payment subsidy deposited into escrow — the difference between the market payment and the reduced payment for each buydown year. Actual costs may vary by lender.

Permanent buydown cost is estimated as the present value of monthly payment savings over the loan term at the market rate. This is an approximation only. Actual discount point pricing changes daily and varies by lender — 1 discount point does not always equal a fixed rate reduction. Contact your lender for actual buydown pricing.
